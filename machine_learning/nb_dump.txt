## Cell 4 — Generator Architecture (G)
Maps a random noise vector **z** ∈ ℝ¹⁰⁰ → a 64×64 RGB image via 5 transposed-convolution blocks. Each block doubles the spatial resolution: `1 → 4 → 8 → 16 → 32 → 64`. Final activation is **Tanh** so output lives in **[-1, 1]**.

## Cell 5 — Discriminator Architecture (D)
Binary classifier: given a 64×64 image outputs **P(real) ∈ [0, 1]**. Spatial dims halve at each block: `64 → 32 → 16 → 8 → 4 → 1`. **LeakyReLU(0.2)** prevents dead neurons and improves gradient flow back to G.

=== Cell 14 (markdown) ===
## Cell 7 — Training Loop
The GAN minimax game over `NUM_EPOCHS` epochs.

**Step A — Train D:** `loss_D = BCELoss(D(x), 1) + BCELoss(D(G(z)), 0)`  
**Step B — Train G:** `loss_G = BCELoss(D(G(z)), 1)`  (G wants D to call its fakes real)

Fixed-noise grids are saved at every epoch end so you can track visual progress.

=== Cell 15 (code) ===
